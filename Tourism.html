Welcome to Uganda

Uganda is a beautiful country located in East Africa and is popularly known as the Pearl of Africa.

Uganda has beautiful national parks, wildlife, mountains, lakes and cultural attractions. Tourists can enjoy activities such as gorilla trekking, hiking, wildlife watching, camping and birdwatching.

About Tourism

Definition of Tourism

Tourism is the activity of travelling from one place to another for leisure, recreation, business, education or visiting attractions.

Impact of COVID-19 on Tourism

COVID-19 greatly affected the tourism sector in Uganda. Travel restrictions reduced the number of tourists visiting the country. Hotels, tour companies and tourism workers lost income.

Solutions to COVID-19 Problems

  • Promoting domestic tourism.
  • Following health and safety guidelines.
  • Using digital marketing.
  • Supporting tourism businesses.
  • Improving tourism infrastructure.
